    The Studio Behind the Work

    Jamaal Treasures is a Florida creative studio for businesses and artists. The studio turns an idea into a finished project, bringing together the visuals, story and digital presence it needs to reach people.

    The studio’s music video directing credits date back to 2018. Working under the CimtexPro name, the studio directed Rayy Dubb’s The Rain, My God and Bang It Out, as well as Rayy Dubb and Jay Million’s Dumb Shit. Those four original YouTube releases have earned more than five million views combined, including releases published by WORLDSTARHIPHOP. More recently, the studio directed 3HREE’s plsdontcallme5ive! and collaborated with Shotby Xay on Rixhkidd4’s Rules nd principals.

    The studio also produces music as Oracle Gemini. The catalog includes Metavision and Echoes In the Ether, and that artist perspective shapes how the studio approaches a video, cover or campaign: the visuals should feel connected to the music and the people behind it.

    For businesses, the studio brings that same attention to the complete picture. Services include creative direction, video production and editing, cinematic AI films, website development, branding and logos, graphic design, social media marketing, and event coverage. Whether you need a new identity, a website that clearly explains your offer, or content for your next launch, the studio connects the pieces around your goal. The studio’s professional background also includes Forbidden Forbes, a business focused on supporting entrepreneurs, small businesses and independent artists through media, branding and marketing.

    Current studio film work includes THE REALM, which brings AI generated imagery together with studio editing. Alongside filmed productions, it gives the studio another way to develop cinematic worlds, explore visual concepts and bring an ambitious idea to the screen.

    Clients work with one point of contact as the idea takes shape, the scope is agreed and the finished work is built. Clients have publicly credited the studio with cover art design and music video editing, and have described a collaborative process that is attentive, creative and easy to be part of. The studio also shares tools and practical guidance through The Treasure Chest, its private creative community.

    Bring the studio your song, your business, your event or the idea you are ready to make real. Every project gets a clear direction and the work to carry it forward.

    Why I made this collection

    Spirituality and imagination are part of how I see the world, and part of everything I create. I’m drawn to that moment when an idea exists only in your mind, full of possibility, before it finds its shape.

    That feeling inspired THE REALM. I wanted to give the inner world a landscape: somewhere ethereal, mysterious, and open to what could be.

    I made this page to share a little of that world with you. These are voices, practices, and creative ideas that connect with my curiosity about who we are and what we can imagine. Take your time. Explore what speaks to you.
