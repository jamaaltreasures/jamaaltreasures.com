Listening counts

Native songs and podcasts have anonymous site listen counts. After 30 seconds of active playback, or 80% of a shorter track, the site records the track and a temporary random play token to avoid duplicate counts. These counters do not store your name, email or IP address and do not identify unique listeners. Cumulative track counts remain; expired play tokens are pruned as later playback starts. Third-party players follow their providers’ privacy policies.
