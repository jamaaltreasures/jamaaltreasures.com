## Research applied

- https://liquidglassdesign.com/prompts — material, lighting, border, and typography references.
- https://liquidglassdesign.com/gallery/liquid-glass-effect-sidebar-ui — navigation hierarchy and layered translucent surfaces.
- https://liquidglassdesign.com/what-is-liquid-glass — distinction between backdrop blur and displacement refraction; contrast and restraint.
- https://liqui.design/docs/handbook/glass — separate frost/refraction layers, canvas-generated displacement maps, browser fallback, layout-size measurement, and reduced-transparency support.
- https://liquidglassdesign.com/resources/liquid-glass-svg-filters — reference approach to SVG material effects.

The site uses an original small vanilla-JavaScript displacement implementation, not copied third-party component code. Live backdrop refraction is enabled on Chromium; other browsers keep the layered frosted material. Copy and interactive content are above, and separate from, the filtered layers. User motion and transparency preferences are respected.
